Welcome to My Teleprompter!

This is my first teleprompter application.

Today, we are building something exciting. A teleprompter application that will help us read scripts while recording videos.

Our application will automatically scroll text upwards at a comfortable reading speed.

We will also be able to pause the scrolling whenever we need to take a break.

Another important feature is the ability to control the scrolling speed.

Some people read slowly while others prefer a faster reading speed.

Eventually, we will be able to enter our own scripts into the application.

We are also planning to make this teleprompter work beautifully on an iPhone.

Thank you for testing My Teleprompter!
